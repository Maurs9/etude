Privacy Policy

Étude is designed as an offline-first practice studio: your practice data, recordings, and imported scores never leave your device.

1. Zero accounts & zero tracking

Étude does not require an account, does not set advertising or analytics cookies, and includes no third-party telemetry, crash reporters, or remote fonts.

2. Data stored on your device

  • Practice progress & settings: spaced-repetition history, reading progress, calibration offsets, and preferences stay in your browser's local storage (ptt-v3 and etude-sight-v1).
  • Safety snapshots & Score Library: automatic rolling backups and imported MusicXML/MXL/MIDI files stay in browser IndexedDB on this device.
  • User control: you can export, restore, or permanently reset your progress and library at any time from Settings.

3. Microphone & MIDI hardware access

  • Microphone: used solely in real time in your browser for sight-singing pitch detection and hum-to-notes transcription. Audio buffers are processed locally in Web Audio and are never uploaded or retained on a server.
  • Web MIDI & Bluetooth MIDI: used solely to exchange live note/pedal events between your connected instrument and the browser.

4. Offline updates & standalone copy

When installed or opened over HTTPS, the Service Worker caches application files locally so Étude works offline and checks only static app files on this origin for updates.
